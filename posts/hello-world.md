Hi, welcome to my first post!

My name is Matteo, and I intend to be using this space to document some of my thoughts and works. I want to use this as a place where I can keep a shareable log of some of the things I work on, learn, and experiment. 

Along the way I would like to share my favorite resources, some of the stuff I have done or will do, and lots of links to some cool stuff, since ["They shall know you through your links"](https://www.sayeverything.com/).  

I do not have one particular topic in mind at the moment, but I will discuss a little bit about life, technology, philosophy, and other stuff. At the moment, I have been interested by the world of open source software, personal finance, (digital and real-life) minimalism, and much more, which I plan to cover in the coming posts.

The inspiration to start this has come from many places: I have been inspired by the many small blogs on [hacker news](https://news.ycombinator.com/), the old italian blogs of [Mr. RIP](https://retireinprogress.com/) and [Salvatore Sanfilippo](https://antirez.com/latest/0), the veteran blogs of [Scott Aaronson](https://scottaaronson.blog/), and [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/), the witty and funny writing of [Matt Levine's Money Stuff](https://www.bloomberg.com/account/newsletters/money-stuff) and many more. 
The inspiration for the layout of the website itself is after [Mitchell Hashimoto](https://mitchellh.com/)'s beautifully simple and clean website.  

If you find this kind of writing valuable and interesting please stick around. Otherwise, at least I got you until here.

Enjoy!
