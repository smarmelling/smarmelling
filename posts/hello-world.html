Hello, World

May 2025

Hello world!
My name is Matteo, and I am a young person trying to find his way through life.
I have decided to open this blog because I feel like I have finally accumulated enough things to share that I thought I would post them in case anyone wants to read.
I do not promise these things will be insightful, innovative, or funny (maybe a little funny), but I can only promise they will be my own unfiltered thoughts on the things I enjoy and do.
I do not have one particular topic in mind but I will discuss life, meaning, technology, sociology, and other stuff. At the moment, I have been interested by the world of open source software, personal finance, (digital and physical) minimalism, and much more, which I plan to cover in the coming posts.
After having long considered opening a blog to post what comes into my head I have decided this is finally the time. Recently, blogs have been my favorite source of weekly reading, and I have thoroughly enjoyed going through old posts of seasoned bloggers to experience their story as if I was reading it as they were writing it for the first time. The biggest inspiration for finally opening a blog has come from many of the small blogs from hacker news, from the italian blogs of Mr. RIP and Salvatore Sanfilippo, and from some old veteran blogs like living a FI and Scott Aaronson.
The inspiration for my style is from Matt Levine's Money Stuff, which I consider the apex of witty writing style to emulate.
The inspiration for the layout of the website itself is created after Mitchell Hashimoto's beautifully simple and clean website.
And many more inspiration that do not currently come to mind or would take too long to list.
If you enjoy any of the sources above I hope you will find some bursts of them in here as well, and if you did not know them before you should give them a try. For me they all really changed my life one way or another.
